Depop fees, explained

How much does Depop take in 2026?

US sellers pay no Depop selling fee. The cost you do pay is payment processing: 3.3% plus $0.45 per sale, on the item price plus shipping and any tax. On a $40 sale with $7.50 buyer-paid shipping and 7% tax, that is $2.13, so $37.87 to you.

How much do Boosted Listings cost?

For US and UK sellers, new listings boosted from 23 March 2026 pay 12% when a boosted listing sells. You pay nothing if it does not sell. The fee is on the item price, and on the shipping cost too if you did not use a Depop shipping label. In the example above, a boosted sale with a Depop label adds $4.80 and leaves you $33.07.

Does Depop charge fees on shipping and tax?

The processing fee does, because it covers the whole order. That is why the percentage on a cheap item looks higher than 3.3%, and why the fixed $0.45 matters most on small sales. Read the "Fees" line above for the real share of your price.

What if I sell outside the US?

This calculator is for US sellers. UK and Australian sellers also pay no selling fee, but processing differs: 2.9% + £0.30 in the UK and 2.6% + A$0.30 in Australia. Sellers elsewhere pay a 10% selling fee plus PayPal's processing fee.

Do I get the fees back on a refund?

Yes. Depop says that when a sale is refunded, its fees, including processing and boosting fees, are reversed too. Sales through Depop Payments reverse automatically.

Rates for Depop US, checked October 2026 on Depop's Help Centre article "Seller fees and charges". Depop can change its fees: check Depop Balance in your account for the exact amount. Sales tax varies by state and is an estimate here. Not affiliated with Depop.
