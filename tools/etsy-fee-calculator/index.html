Etsy fees, explained

How much does Etsy take per sale?

On a typical US sale Etsy takes a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping, a payment processing fee of 3% of the order total plus $0.25, and $0.20 to relist each item sold. Together that is usually about 10-12% of the order, more if the sale came through Offsite Ads.

What is the Offsite Ads fee?

If a buyer clicks an Etsy ad on another site and buys within 30 days, Etsy charges 15% of the order (12% for shops that made $10,000 or more in the past year), capped at $100 per order.

Is the transaction fee charged on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee applies to the item price and the shipping you charge. Payment processing also applies to sales tax collected on the order.

How do I price to make a profit on Etsy?

Add up your materials, postage and Etsy's fees, then work backward from the margin you want. The "price for your target margin" result above does that for you.
