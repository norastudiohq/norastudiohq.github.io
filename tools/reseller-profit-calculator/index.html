Reseller fees, explained

Which platform has the lowest fees for resellers?

For a typical $40 item, Depop (US: payment processing only, 3.3% + $0.45) and Mercari (10%) usually take the least, eBay takes about 13.6% of the item plus shipping plus $0.40, and Poshmark takes the most at 20% (a flat $2.95 under $15). The lowest fee is not always the most profit: the platform where the item actually sells at your price wins.

How do I calculate profit on a flip?

Profit = sale price + shipping the buyer paid - platform fees - postage - what you paid for the item - other costs such as cleaning or supplies. ROI is that profit divided by everything you spent on the item.

What is the most I should pay for an item to resell?

Start from the price it realistically sells for, subtract the platform's fees, postage, other costs and the profit you want. What's left is your maximum buy price. The "Most to pay" column does that for every platform.

Does eBay charge fees on shipping?

Yes. eBay's final value fee applies to the total the buyer pays, including shipping (and sales tax), so charging more for shipping does not dodge the fee. Mercari, Depop and Etsy also charge on shipping.
