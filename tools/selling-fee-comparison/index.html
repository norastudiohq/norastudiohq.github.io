Selling fees compared, explained

Which platform has the lowest selling fees in 2026?

It depends on the price, which is why the table above ranks them for your item instead of naming one winner. Whatnot's 8% commission plus 2.9% processing is the lowest headline rate of the five, and Depop charges US sellers no selling fee at all, only 3.3% + $0.45 processing. Poshmark's 20% is the highest percentage, but its flat $2.95 under $15 makes it the cheapest option on genuinely cheap items. eBay sits at 13.6% of the whole order in most categories, and Mercari at 10% of the item plus buyer-paid shipping.

Poshmark vs Mercari: which pays more?

Mercari's 10% beats Poshmark's 20% on anything from about $15 up, and the gap widens with price. Below $15 Poshmark's flat $2.95 can win, because 20% never applies: on a $10 sale Poshmark takes $2.95 while Mercari takes 10% of the item plus the shipping the buyer paid. Enter your own numbers above rather than going on the headline rates, because Mercari charges on shipping and Poshmark does not.

Do these platforms charge fees on shipping and sales tax?

They differ, and it changes the answer. Poshmark charges on the item price only. Mercari charges 10% on the item price plus buyer-paid shipping, but not tax. eBay's final value fee, Depop's processing fee and Whatnot's processing fee all apply to the full order total, which includes shipping and the sales tax the buyer paid. Whatnot's commission, unlike its processing fee, is on the item price only.

Why does a cheap item lose a bigger share to fees?

Because of the fixed charges. eBay adds $0.30 or $0.40 per order, Depop $0.45 and Whatnot $0.30, and those do not shrink with the price. On a $5 sale a $0.40 order fee is 8% on its own. The "Fee % of price" column shows the real share for your price, not the headline rate.

What is not in this comparison?

Promoted or boosted listings beyond the Depop toggle, eBay Store subscriptions and their lower rates, Poshmark Promoted Closet, seller-paid shipping discounts, Mercari's ad fees, Whatnot's volume tiers below 8%, and anything a one-off promotion changes. Each platform's own calculator on this site covers more of its specifics. International selling is not covered: all five charge US sellers differently from sellers elsewhere.

Which one should I actually list on?

Fees are rarely the deciding factor. A platform where the item sells in a week at $40 beats one where it sits for three months at $45. Use this table to rule out the option that quietly costs you $8 more on the same sale, then list where your category has buyers. If you cross-list, the Reseller Tracker above keeps the real per-platform numbers so you can see which one actually pays you best over a month.
