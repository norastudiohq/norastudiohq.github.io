eBay fees, explained

How much does eBay take in 2026?

For most categories, a final value fee of 13.6% of the total amount of the sale (up to $7,500, then 2.35% on the rest) plus a per-order fee of $0.30 on orders of $10 or less and $0.40 on orders over $10. On a $40 item with free shipping and 7% sales tax, that is $5.82 + $0.40 = $6.22, so after a $7.50 label you keep $26.28.

Does eBay charge fees on shipping and sales tax?

Yes. eBay's "total amount of the sale" includes the item price, handling, shipping the buyer pays, sales tax and other fees. That is why this calculator asks for a tax rate. The tax itself goes to the state, so it is not part of your payout, but the fee on it is. We apply your rate to price plus shipping; some states do not tax shipping, so your exact fee can be a few cents lower.

Which categories have different fees?

Books, movies and music pay 15.3%. Trading cards, comic books and coins pay 13.25%. Athletic sneakers pay 8% with no per-order fee when the sale is $150 or more, and 13.6% below that. Women's handbags pay 15% up to $2,000 and jewelry 15% up to $5,000 (9% above). Watches pay 15% up to $1,000, 6.5% from $1,000 to $7,500 and 3% above. Guitars and basses pay 6.7%.

Is free shipping or charging shipping better on eBay?

The fee is on the total either way, so fees barely change: charging $7.50 shipping on a $40 item, or listing at $47.50 with free shipping, costs about the same. Choose whatever sells faster in your category.

What is the lowest offer I should accept?

Add the profit you want to what you paid and your other costs, then find the price whose payout reaches that number. The calculator does this for you in the "Lowest offer" line.

What is not included?

Insertion fees (most sellers get 250 free listings a month, then $0.35), promoted listing ad fees, eBay Store subscription discounts, the international fee and extra fees for sellers below performance standards. Add promoted listing costs under "Other costs" if you use them.

Fee rates for eBay US non-Store sellers, checked October 2026 on eBay's "Selling fees" help page. eBay can change its fees: check the fee breakdown on your order for the exact amount.
