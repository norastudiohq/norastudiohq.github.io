Poshmark fees, explained

How much does Poshmark take in 2026?

In the US, Poshmark charges a flat $2.95 on sales under $15 and 20% of the sale price on sales of $15 or more. There is no listing fee and no monthly fee, and the buyer pays for the prepaid shipping label. A $35 sale costs a $7.00 fee, so you earn $28.00.

Why is the fee so high on cheap items?

The $2.95 flat fee is a big share of a small sale: on a $10 item it is 29.5%, and you earn $7.05. A $15 sale costs $3.00 (20%), so a $14 item and a $15 item earn almost the same ($11.05 vs $12.00). Bundling cheap items into one sale is the usual fix.

What is the lowest offer I should accept?

Work back from the profit you want: add it to what you paid, your other costs and any shipping discount you cover, then find the price whose payout reaches that number. The calculator does this for you in the "Lowest offer" line.

What price should I list at to earn a set amount?

For payouts above $12.05, divide the payout you want by 0.8 (for $30, list at $37.50). For smaller payouts, add $2.95. Buyers often offer 10-30% below the listing price, so leave room.

Does Poshmark charge fees on shipping?

No. The fee is on the sale price. If you offer a shipping discount, the part of the label you cover comes out of your earnings.

Fee rates for Poshmark US, checked October 2026. Other countries use different rates. Poshmark can change its fees: check your sale summary for the exact amount.
