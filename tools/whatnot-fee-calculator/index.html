Whatnot fees, explained

How much does Whatnot take in 2026?

Two fees come out of each sale. Commission starts at 8% of the item's final sale price (Coins & Money starts at 4%). The payment processing fee is 2.9% of the total order value plus $0.30 per order. On a $40 win with $7.50 buyer-paid shipping and 7% tax, that is $3.20 commission plus $1.77 processing, so $4.97 in fees and $35.03 to you.

Does Whatnot charge fees on shipping and tax?

Commission does not: it applies to the item price only. The processing fee does, because it is based on the total order value. That is why a cheap item with buyer-paid shipping costs more in fees, as a share of the price, than the headline percentage suggests.

What happens on a $1 start?

The $0.30 per-order charge does not shrink with the price. Enter your own shipping and costs, then read the "Break-even bid" line: that is the lowest winning bid that covers your fees and what you paid. Below it, the sale loses money.

How do I get a lower commission?

Whatnot lowers the rate as sales grow, starting at $15,000 in a 4-week period, and resets it every four weeks. Tier rates vary by category. Put your own rate in the Commission box if you have one. Whatnot says custom rates go as low as 3% for select high-volume sellers.

What if I pay the shipping myself?

Untick "Buyer pays shipping". The label then comes out of your payout, and it is no longer part of the order total, so the processing fee drops slightly.

Rates for Whatnot US, checked October 2026 on whatnot.com/seller/fees. Whatnot can change its fees and promotions may lower them: check your order summary in Seller Hub for the exact amount. Sales tax varies by state and is an estimate here.
